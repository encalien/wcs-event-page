Final Information

Only a few days left before we start our West Coast Spring weekend. In this email you'll find some important information regarding the coming event. Please read them carefully, and if you have any questions, don't hesitate to ask on Facebook event or via email.

Check in

Check-in will be possible at the party on:

  • Friday night between 19:45 and 00:00 and
  • Saturday before workshops between 11:30 and 12:00.

We only need your name for the check-in. If you haven't sorted out your payment yet, you can also do that in cash upon arrival, but we prefer if you pay in advance. If you already paid, but have not received a payment confirmation email, please bring proof of payment with you.

Each party has an entrance fee of 5 EUR. Both parties are included in full pass.

Location

The entire weekend program will happen in the dance school BastArts Academy, located on Ob železnici 14, 1000 Ljubljana.

As you arrive you will pass a parking barrier. The barrier is down during the night, so to open it going in, please call +38651244522 (Ivi, BastArts Academy). Going out, please try to leave in groups to minimize the number of times the barrier needs to be opened.

Immediately after the barrier to the right, you will see a big parking lot. Please park there, and not on the reserved parking spots in front of the entrance or on the driveway.

Venue rules and info

As we're hosting the event in a new venue, there are some additional rules to follow:

  • leaning on walls or mirrors is strictly forbidden
  • no food or drinks (except bottled water) are allowed in the dance area
  • any spillage must be cleaned up immediately or reported to the staff

The venue does not have a bar, but we will provide some light snacks and soft drinks for sale during the parties. You're welcome to bring your own drinks and snacks, but please be mindful of the venue rules.

Health and security

We're planning to comply with any health recommendations that may be in place at the time of the event. We kindly ask that you do not attend the event in case you're showing symptoms of infectious disease or have tested positive for the new coronavirus. We will also provide sanitizers, which we encourage you to use regularly.

Please take care of your personal belongings and don't leave them unattended, we take no responsibility for any lost or stolen items.

You participate in the event at your own risk.
